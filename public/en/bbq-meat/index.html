How the planner works

  • Each adult: about 250 g (light), 350 g (normal) or 500 g (very hungry) of raw meat.
  • A child counts as half an adult.
  • Amounts are rounded to a quarter of a kilo, with at least 0.25 kg of each meat.

These amounts come from the working version. The butcher will confirm them.

Questions and answers

Can I order a larger amount?

Send us the date, how many people and what you'd like. We agree quantities and timing based on what's available.

Why is the total only an estimate?

Anything sold by weight is charged on what it actually weighs, at the confirmed price per kilo. Got a budget limit? Say so in the enquiry.
