Questions and answers

Can I request a larger quantity?

To check options for a larger gathering, send your date, guest count and preferred selection. Quantities and timing are agreed according to availability.

Is the meat ready to eat?

Check each product's description. Fresh meat and raw meat products require cooking; check whether prepared dishes need cooking or reheating.

Why is the total an estimate?

For products sold by weight, the final amount depends on the actual quantity weighed and the confirmed price per kilogram. Let us know if you have a budget limit.
