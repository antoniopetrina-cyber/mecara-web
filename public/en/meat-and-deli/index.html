Questions and answers

How can I check a product's availability?

Send the product name, approximate quantity and preferred date. We will check the current selection and let you know what is available.

Can I request a particular steak thickness?

Include your preferred thickness or cutting instructions in the enquiry. We will check whether this is possible for the cut you have chosen.

Do you offer gluten-free products?

Ask which products have verified gluten-free labelling. Before buying, check the ingredients, allergens and handling information for the specific item.

Is the meat ready to eat?

Check each product's description. Fresh meat and raw meat products require cooking; check whether prepared dishes need cooking or reheating.

Can I request a larger quantity?

To check options for a larger gathering, send your date, guest count and preferred selection. Quantities and timing are agreed according to availability.
