Fresh meat and deli favourites in Dubrovnik

Pick the meat for the dish, not the other way round. A stew, a roast, a peka, a quick pan fry and a barbecue all want different cuts. Come to Mecara in Lapad and ask which.

Everyday cooking and special dinners

Beef, veal, lamb, pork and chicken are all below, so have a look at what's in. Need a particular cut, or a lot of something? Get in touch before you come. Tell us if you want it as a whole piece, in portions or cut for a specific dish.

Cured meats, cheese and deli bits

Something to pick at before the main event. We have prosciutto, other cured meats and cheese. The producer, origin and ingredients are listed with each product.

Questions and answers

How do I know if you've got something?

Send us the product, roughly how much and the date you want it. We'll check and tell you what's available.

Can I ask for a specific thickness?

Put the thickness or how you want it cut in your enquiry. We'll tell you if that works for the piece you picked.

Anything gluten-free?

Ask which products carry a verified gluten-free label. Before you buy, check the ingredients, allergens and handling information for that item.

Is the meat ready to eat?

No. Fresh meat and raw meat products need cooking. For prepared dishes, check the description to see whether they need cooking or just reheating.

Can I order a larger amount?

Send us the date, how many people and what you'd like. We agree quantities and timing based on what's available.
