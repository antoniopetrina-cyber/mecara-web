Mecara, your butcher shop in Lapad

Mecara is for people who like to know what they're buying and how they'll cook it. We're inside the DOC shopping centre in Dubrovnik, and most conversations at our counter start the same way. What are you making today?

We start with the meal

Sometimes it's a cut for slow cooking, sometimes a couple of steaks, sometimes enough meat for the whole group. Tell us the dish, how many you're feeding and what you're cooking on, and we'll help you choose.

Ask about the product

Ask about the origin, ingredients and availability of the thing you want. A straight answer beats guessing from a name or a photo.

Visit us at DOC

Have a look at what's in, or send us your list. For specific cuts or larger amounts, check with us first.
