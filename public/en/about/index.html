Mecara, your butcher shop in Lapad

Mecara is for people who want to know what they are buying and how to cook it. Find us inside the DOC shopping centre in Dubrovnik, where choosing a good meal can start with a simple question: what are you making today?

Choose around the meal

You may need a cut for slow cooking, a few steaks or enough meat for a whole group. Tell us about the dish, your guest count and your cooking equipment so we can help with your choice.

Ask about the product

Check the origin, ingredients and availability of the item you want to buy. A clear answer about a particular product is more useful than guessing from its name or photo.

Visit us at DOC

Browse the current selection or contact us with your list. For specific cuts and larger quantities, it is best to check in advance.
