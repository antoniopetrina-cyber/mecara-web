Villas & yachts · English and Croatian

Meat for villas and yachts in the Dubrovnik area

Barbecue at the villa, dinner on board, or a shopping list for your private chef? Send us the meat and deli items you want, the number of guests and the date. We'll check what we have and come back with a selection.

Request a quote for your group

Prepared for your kitchen

List the cuts, quantities and how you'd like them packed. Tell us if it's a barbecue, a peka or a steak dinner, what equipment you have, and when you're cooking.

Location and timing

Give us the villa address, or the yacht name, marina and berth. Whether we can deliver, what it costs and when are confirmed case by case. If delivery isn't possible, we can talk about collection from the shop.

Confirmed before we prepare

Before we prepare anything, we agree the products, any substitutions, the price or weight-based calculation, and collection or delivery. Sending the form doesn't confirm a booking.
