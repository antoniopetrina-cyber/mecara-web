Adults and children, bone-in cuts, starters and side dishes all affect how much you may need. Treat the result as a starting point, not a fixed rule for every barbecue.

How the planner works

  • Each adult: about 250 g (light), 350 g (normal) or 500 g (very hungry) of raw meat.
  • A child counts as half an adult portion.
  • Amounts are rounded to a quarter of a kilogram, with at least 0.25 kg per meat.

These amounts are a working-version suggestion and will be confirmed by the butcher.

Questions and answers

Can I request a larger quantity?

To check options for a larger gathering, send your date, guest count and preferred selection. Quantities and timing are agreed according to availability.

Why is the total an estimate?

For products sold by weight, the final amount depends on the actual quantity weighed and the confirmed price per kilogram. Let us know if you have a budget limit.
