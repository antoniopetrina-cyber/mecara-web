Which cut suits your dinner?

Would you prefer a bone-in steak or a boneless cut? Are you cooking individual portions or sharing a larger piece? Tell us your plans and ask about the differences between cuts such as rib-eye, T-bone and striploin.

Check the details of your cut

Questions and answers

Do you offer dry-aged steaks?

Check the dry-aged steak page or ask the butcher about the current selection. Confirm the cut, ageing details and weight before visiting for a specific piece.

Can I request a particular steak thickness?

Include your preferred thickness or cutting instructions in the enquiry. We will check whether this is possible for the cut you have chosen.

Why is the total an estimate?

For products sold by weight, the final amount depends on the actual quantity weighed and the confirmed price per kilogram. Let us know if you have a budget limit.
