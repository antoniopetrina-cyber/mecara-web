Questions and answers

Where is Mecara?

Inside the DOC shopping centre in Lapad, at Kralja Tomislava 7, Dubrovnik. Use the directions link to find the location.

Is my order confirmed when I click the button?

No. The button prepares an enquiry for you to send. Mecara confirms the order and timing in a separate message.

Why is the total an estimate?

For products sold by weight, the final amount depends on the actual quantity weighed and the confirmed price per kilogram. Let us know if you have a budget limit.

Can I collect at my preferred time?

Include your preferred collection time. Collection is arranged only after we confirm availability and timing.

Is parking free?

Customers get one hour of free parking at the DOC and Tommy car park.
