Questions and answers

Where are you?

In the DOC shopping centre in Lapad, Kralja Tomislava 7, Dubrovnik. The directions link will get you here.

Is my order confirmed when I click?

No. The button only puts the enquiry together for you to send. Mecara confirms the order and time in a separate message.

Why is the total only an estimate?

Anything sold by weight is charged on what it actually weighs, at the confirmed price per kilo. Got a budget limit? Say so in the enquiry.

Can I collect when it suits me?

Give us your preferred time in the enquiry. It's only set once we've confirmed availability and timing.

Is parking free?

For an hour, yes. Customers park free for one hour at the DOC and Tommy car park.
